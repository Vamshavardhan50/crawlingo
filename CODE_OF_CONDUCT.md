# Community code of conduct

Crawlingo welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces
managed by the maintainers.

## Working together

- Discuss code, evidence and user needs respectfully.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept corrections.
- Respect privacy; share only information needed to reproduce a problem.
- Keep discussions relevant and make room for new contributors.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate
disruption and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close disruptive discussions or restrict
participation to protect the community. They should explain the relevant behavior
when doing so, while respecting confidential reports and the people involved.

Use GitHub's reporting tools on the relevant content for abuse or privacy concerns.
Avoid posting private details in a public issue. For a project moderation request,
ask the repository owner for a private contact route if one is not listed.

Apply the same standards to maintainers and contributors. Reports should describe
specific conduct and context; disagreement over a technical decision is not itself
a conduct violation.
